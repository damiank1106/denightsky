DE Night Sky Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

DE Night Sky is an invitation-only app for a private family Allsky camera archive and its Raspberry Pi. This policy explains the account, media, telemetry, and operational information used to provide the service.

Information the app may process

Depending on the features enabled for your account and server, DE Night Sky may process:

  • Your account email, role, and mobile-session information needed to authenticate you and enforce administrator, family-viewer, or Apple-reviewer permissions.
  • Camera and system information, including live or stored sky imagery, camera status, storage information, Raspberry Pi status, and service health.
  • Environmental and equipment telemetry, including temperature, humidity, dew point, dew margin, heater mode/state, and related operational values.
  • Administrator-only actions, such as recording/day deletion or a heater-mode request, together with security and equipment-event logs.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide, secure, diagnose, and operate DE Night Sky and the connected Allsky system. DE Night Sky does not sell personal information, display advertising, or use third-party tracking analytics.

Authentication and local storage

Every approved account uses the same native email-and-password sign-in screen and the same rate-limited DE Night Sky authentication endpoint. The backend stores passwords only as salted Argon2id hashes with a unique random salt per account; plaintext passwords are never embedded in the app. Successful authentication issues short-lived app access credentials and rotating refresh credentials, which are stored only in the iOS Keychain and sent only over encrypted HTTPS. Cloudflare Tunnel carries traffic to the private Raspberry Pi service without exposing a public Pi port. Signing out revokes the local refresh session. A password change revokes every active app session for that account and requires a fresh sign-in.

A bounded thumbnail cache and app preferences may remain on the device until removed by iOS or the user. Removing the app does not automatically delete server-side media or logs.

Private family media

Sky images, videos, Startrails, keograms, events, and related metadata are private family content. They are available only to approved, authenticated accounts and are not intended for public indexing or advertising use.

Downloads and sharing

Media is downloaded only when you request it. When you save or share an item, iOS may copy it to Photos, Files, another app, or a recipient you select. Those copies are then governed by your choices and the destination's privacy practices.

Roles and administrator actions

Family and Apple-reviewer accounts are read-only. Only the exact configured administrator may request deletion of eligible recordings or change heater mode. The backend enforces these permissions and may record administrator, authentication, deletion, and equipment events in operational logs. The administrator can see a safe read-only account list containing email, role, and enabled state; passwords and password hashes are never displayed.

Apple review access

A dedicated, revocable Apple-reviewer account uses the same sign-in screen and session protections as every other account. It is viewer-only and cannot delete media, control the heater, manage accounts, or perform other administrator actions.

Retention

Authentication and session records, operational logs, archive retention, and deletion records are managed by the private DE Night Sky services. No fixed retention period is promised; contact us with a privacy or deletion question.

Children

This is an invitation-only private family service and is not directed to the general public. A parent or guardian should manage access for any minor family member.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, backend, or data practices change. This page will show the current effective date.
