DE Night Sky Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

DE Night Sky is an app for authorized users to view and manage a private Allsky camera system and its associated Raspberry Pi hardware. This policy explains the information the app processes and how that information is used.

Information the app may process

Depending on the features enabled for your account and server, DE Night Sky may process:

  • Account and session information needed to authenticate you and determine your role, such as administrator, family viewer, or reviewer.
  • Camera and system information, including live or stored sky imagery, camera status, storage information, Raspberry Pi status, and service health.
  • Environmental and equipment telemetry, including temperature, humidity, dew point, heater state, and related operational values.
  • Authorized control actions, such as administrator commands that change supported device settings or delete permitted data.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide, secure, diagnose, and operate DE Night Sky and the connected Allsky system. DE Night Sky does not sell personal information and is not designed to use personal information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Authentication and local storage

Authentication credentials or session tokens used by the app may be stored securely on your Apple device using platform security features such as Keychain. Removing the app removes app-local data, but information stored on the connected Raspberry Pi or server may remain until it is deleted there.

Connected system and infrastructure

DE Night Sky communicates with the Raspberry Pi and server endpoints configured for the deployment. Network and access infrastructure providers, including Cloudflare when used by the deployment, may process network and security metadata according to their own privacy policies and service terms.

Data retention and deletion

Operational data, images, and logs are retained according to the settings of the connected Allsky/Raspberry Pi system. Where administrator deletion features are available, authorized administrators can remove supported data from that system.

Security

DE Night Sky is designed to use authenticated access, encrypted network connections, role-based permissions, and Apple platform security features. No system can guarantee absolute security, so access credentials should be kept private and devices should remain updated.

Children

DE Night Sky is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for authorized users of a privately managed camera system.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when DE Night Sky features or data-handling practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support information provided with your DE Night Sky deployment or the support contact listed on the app's App Store product page.
